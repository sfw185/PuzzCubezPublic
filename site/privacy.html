Privacy

PuzzCubez, 24 October 2026

PuzzCubez collects no data about you. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it makes no network connections.

The game saves your progress (the furthest level you have reached, and which arrows are gone from a level you are part way through) in its own storage on your device. It never leaves the device, except within your device's own backups, such as iCloud Backup, which Apple's terms cover. Deleting the app deletes it.

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.
