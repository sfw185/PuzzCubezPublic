Privacy

PuzzCubez for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, updated 28 November 2026

PuzzCubez collects no data about you. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and no servers of its own, so nothing you do in it ever reaches us. On Android it asks for one permission only, to vibrate for haptics.

The game saves your progress (the furthest level you have reached, the levels and daily cubes you have solved, and which arrows are gone from a cube you are part way through) in its own storage on your device, along with your settings.

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, if you are signed in to iCloud, the game also keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account, so it is the same on each of your devices. Apple stores it under your account, as its iCloud terms cover, and we never see it. Your settings stay on each device.

Otherwise your progress leaves the device only within your device's own backups, such as iCloud Backup or Android's backup to your Google Account, which Apple's and Google's terms cover. Deleting the app deletes the copy on that device.

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.
