PuzzCubez

Support

A cube covered in arrows. Tap one and it slides off along its path, as long as nothing is in its way. Clear every arrow to finish the level.

How to play

Drag to turn the cube, pinch to zoom, and drag with two fingers to slide it. If an arrow is blocked it bumps, and the arrow in its way flashes. The lightbulb lights up an arrow that can leave; the other button brings the whole cube back into view. On Android, going back takes you to the start menu.

Which devices can I play on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon through the Mac App Store. The Android version, for phones, tablets and foldables with Android 8 or later, is coming soon to Google Play.

Can I get stuck?

No. A blocked arrow never moves, so no tap can make a level impossible.

My progress

Progress is saved as you play, on your device. Tap the level title to play any level you have finished again.
