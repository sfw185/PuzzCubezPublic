PuzzCubez

Untangle the arrow cube.

Coming soon to the App Store

Tap an arrow and it slides off the cube along its path, as long as its way out is clear. If another arrow is in the way, it bumps and the blocker flashes. Work out the order, and clear every arrow.

  • 100 levels, from a single arrow to 237 long, twisting ones that wind over the cube's edges
  • You can't get stuck: no wrong moves, no lives, no timers
  • Each side turns its own colour once you clear it
  • Pay once. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no tracking
